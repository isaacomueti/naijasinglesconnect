Private Circle

For members living with HIV

Many people living with HIV are healthy, on treatment and ready for marriage. The Private Circle lets you meet others who understand, without explaining your story to anyone else.

  • Opt in privately from your profile, and leave at any time
  • You are matched only with members who have also joined
  • Nobody outside the circle can see you or your status

Membership

Simple membership. Human support.

One clear fee, a real team behind it, and a community where everyone is here for the same reason.

One membership fee, paid by bank transfer. Upload your receipt from your dashboard and we confirm it. Card and USSD payments through Paystack or Flutterwave are coming soon.

Questions go to a real person, in your dashboard or on WhatsApp, Monday to Saturday from 9am to 7pm. Most members are approved the same day.

Every member states that they are looking for marriage, and every profile is reviewed before they can see anyone or be seen.

Questions

Before you join

Still unsure? Message us from your dashboard and a person will answer.

NSC is only for people looking for marriage. Every profile is read by a person before it goes live, matches are ranked by shared values and plans rather than photos, and you choose how much of yourself other members can see.

A member of the NSC team reads every new profile, every photo and every payment receipt. Profiles that look fake, incomplete or not serious about marriage are declined or sent back with a note explaining what to change.

No. Members of every faith are welcome. Your religion is part of your profile, and you can choose to be matched only with people who share it.

Yes. Nigerians and people of Nigerian heritage can join wherever they live, including the UK, US, Canada and across Africa.

Yes. If you hope to marry a Nigerian, you are welcome. Add your nationality to your profile and you will be matched with Nigerians who are open to meeting people from other countries.

Yes. The Private Circle is an optional, private space for members living with HIV. If you join, you are matched only with other members who have joined, and nobody outside the circle can see you or your status. You can join or leave at any time from your profile.

Your deal-breakers are applied first, so anyone who fails one never appears. Everyone left is ranked by how closely your faith, marriage plans, family hopes, age range and location line up.

Genotype compatibility matters for the health of your future children. We flag pairings that carry a risk of sickle cell disease so you can talk about it early. It is only visible to members you are matched with.

Usually within 24 hours of your payment being confirmed. You can message the team from your dashboard if you have been waiting longer.
