Questions

Before you join

Who can join NaijaSinglesConnect?

Singles of any faith who are Nigerian or of Nigerian heritage, aged 21 and over, and who are seriously looking for marriage. Members can live in Nigeria or abroad.

Is NaijaSinglesConnect only for Christians?

No. Members of every faith are welcome. Your religion is part of your profile, and you can choose to be matched only with people who share it, and we treat that as a deal-breaker.

Why do you ask for my genotype?

Genotype compatibility matters for the health of your future children. We use it to flag pairings that carry a risk of sickle cell disease, so you can talk about it early. It is only visible to members you are matched with.

Who checks the profiles?

A member of the NaijaSinglesConnect team reads every new profile and every change to photos or descriptions. We decline profiles that look fake, incomplete or not serious about marriage.

Can people from my place of worship see that I am here?

Only approved members can see profiles. If you want more privacy, choose the name-restricted or no-photo setting and you will appear with your initials or without photographs.

How long does approval take?

Usually within 24 hours of your payment being confirmed. Message us on WhatsApp if you have been waiting longer.
