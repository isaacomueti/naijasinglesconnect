Trust & safety

Designed for intentional dating. Built with safety in mind.

Answers to the questions you're already asking: are these real people, is my information private, and what happens if someone behaves badly?

Every profile reviewed

A real person on our team reads every profile and photo before it goes live, and declines anything that looks fake or unserious.

Privacy controls

Show your full profile, hide your photos, or show only your initials until you choose to connect. Your phone and email are never shown.

Report and block

Report or block anyone in one tap from their profile or your conversation. Our team reviews every report.

Messages only after a yes

Nobody can message you until you both accept an introduction, so there are no unwanted messages.

Community standards

Everyone here is looking for marriage. Asking for money, harassment, or lying about your marital status gets an account paused.

Real people to talk to

Questions or concerns go to a real person on our team, from your dashboard, Monday to Saturday.

Safety tip: never send money or share bank details with someone you've met online. Keep conversations on NSC until you're comfortable.

Questions

Before you join

Still unsure? Message us from your dashboard and a person will answer.

NSC is only for people looking for marriage. Every profile is read by a person before it goes live, matches are ranked by shared faith, values and plans rather than photos, and you choose how much of yourself other members can see.

Creating your account and building your profile is free and takes about 10 minutes.

A member of the NSC team reads every new profile and every photo before it goes live. Profiles that look fake, incomplete or not serious about marriage are declined or sent back with a note explaining what to change.

No. Members of every faith are welcome. Your religion is part of your profile, and you can choose to be matched only with people who share it.

Yes. Nigerians and people of Nigerian heritage can join wherever they live. If you're not Nigerian but hope to marry a Nigerian, you're welcome too, and you'll be matched with Nigerians who are open to meeting people from other countries.

Your deal-breakers are applied first, so anyone who fails one never appears. Everyone left is ranked by how closely your faith, marriage plans, family hopes, age range and location line up.

Yes. The Private Circle is an optional, private space. If you join, you're matched only with other members who have joined, and nobody outside the circle can see you or your status. You can join or leave at any time from your profile.

Genotype compatibility matters for the health of your future children. We flag pairings that carry a risk of sickle cell disease so you can talk about it early. It's only visible to members you're matched with.
