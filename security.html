Security policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version
  • Your account, messages and receipts are protected at every step, and members never see your full name, phone number or email.
  • Every chat message is checked for scams. Bank details and links can't be shared between members.
  • Our team will never ask for your password or a code we send you.
  • Found a security problem? Email support@naijasinglesconnect.com and we'll respond within 5 working days.

1. How we protect your account

  • Encrypted connections. Everything between your device and NSC travels over HTTPS.
  • Confirmed sign-ups. New accounts are confirmed with a code sent to the member's email.
  • Strict access rules in the database. Every request is checked against who is asking, so one member can never read another member's private details, messages or receipts, whatever the website shows.
  • Protection from automated attacks. Our forms quietly screen out bots, and repeated attempts are limited.
  • A locked-down website. Our pages only load code from NSC and a small list of trusted providers, and can't be embedded in other sites.
  • A small, reviewed team. Only named team members can open the admin dashboard, and every action they take is recorded.

2. Who can see what

  • Other members see your first name and the first letter of your last name, your photos (following your privacy setting) and your profile answers.
  • Your full name, phone number, email address and payment details are never shown to other members.
  • Private Circle membership is visible only to other circle members and our review team.
  • Payment receipts are stored privately and can only be opened by you and our team, through links that expire.

Our Privacy policy explains in full what we collect and why.

3. Safe messaging

Romance scams usually start by moving the conversation elsewhere or asking for money. To prevent this, every message between members is checked as it is sent:

  • Bank details and links can't be sent.
  • Messages that mention money, phone numbers or abusive language are delivered but shown to our team, who may step in.
  • Sending is limited so no one can flood other members.
  • You can report or block anyone from their profile or your conversation at any time.

4. Payments

Membership is paid by bank transfer to Best of Nature Ventures, NSC's parent company. Our team checks every receipt against the bank statement before a membership starts, and a payment for less than the plan price is never accepted. NSC will never ask you to pay into any other account, and no member will ever be asked to pay another member.

5. Keeping yourself safe

  • Use a strong password you don't use anywhere else, and never share the codes we email you.
  • Our team will never ask for your password, your sign-in code or your bank PIN.
  • Never send money, gifts or airtime to someone you have met online, however convincing the story.
  • Only sign in at naijasinglesconnect.com. Be wary of look-alike addresses.

6. Reporting a security problem

If you believe you've found a weakness in NSC, please tell us privately at support@naijasinglesconnect.com with "Security" in the subject. Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it and any page addresses involved. We will acknowledge your report within 5 working days, keep you updated while we fix it, and credit you if you'd like.

For problems with another member, such as a scam, harassment or a fake profile, use the Report button on their profile or in your conversation instead. Our team reviews every report.

7. Rules for security research

We welcome good-faith research that follows these rules:

  • Only test with accounts you created yourself, and never access, change or delete another member's information.
  • Don't attempt to overload the service, send spam, or use social engineering on members or our team.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix a problem before sharing it with anyone else.

We won't pursue action against anyone who follows these rules in good faith.
