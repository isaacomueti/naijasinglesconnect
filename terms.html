2. Who can join

You can use NSC if all of these are true:

  • You are at least 21 years old.
  • You are single: never married, divorced, widowed, or legally separated and free to marry under the law that applies to you.
  • You are looking for a relationship that leads to marriage.
  • You have not been convicted of a violent or sexual offence, and you have not been removed from NSC before.

NSC welcomes people of every faith, Nigerians at home and abroad, and people of any nationality who hope to marry a Nigerian.

Approval isn't a background check: we don't verify identity documents, criminal records or marital status, so please take the care described in section 8.

6. Community standards

If someone breaks these rules, report them from their profile or your conversation. Our team reviews every report.
